AstroMation — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

AstroMation (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an astrology app published by Machinity (info@machinity.ai). This policy explains what information the app processes and how. We designed AstroMation to keep your personal data on your device by default.

1. Information the app processes

  • Birth data you enter — birth date, birth time, and birth place (city or latitude/longitude). For people you add, their name and the same birth details.
  • App preferences — language, theme, and your subscription/entitlement status.

We do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts.

2. On-device processing and storage

Your birth chart is calculated entirely on your device. The birth data and the profiles you save are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. This data is not uploaded to us or to any server as part of normal chart calculation.

3. AI interpretations

When you request an AI-written reading (for example a natal reading, a daily reading, or an answer in the Ask feature), the app sends the chart data needed for that interpretation — such as planetary positions, aspects, transit contacts, the language you chose, and the question or context you typed — over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our authenticated reading gateway, which calls OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/privacy), which routes the request to the large-language-model provider that generates the text. We send only what is needed to produce the reading. Selected profile context may be included; we do not send your saved people list. Firebase App Check verifies the app, and our gateway applies usage and spending limits. These requests may be processed on servers located outside your country.

Optional place search sends your place query to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. You can enter coordinates instead. Neither place search nor AI is required to calculate a chart.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by the Apple App Store and Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details. The app only learns whether an entitlement is active, in order to unlock premium features. Subscriptions are auto-renewable; you can manage or cancel them in your App Store or Google Play account settings. See our Terms of Use for details.

5. Rewarded advertising and optional analytics

From version 1.2.9, the free mobile app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads to unlock new AI natal readings, daily readings, and advisor answers. You choose whether to watch an ad. Premium users do not see these ads. Saved readings and on-device chart calculations remain accessible without watching another ad.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostics to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized; this does not mean that no advertising data is processed. We do not supply your birth data, charts, questions, or readings to AdMob. See Google's privacy policy.

Google's User Messaging Platform manages advertising consent where applicable. Advertising privacy choices are available in Settings when required in your region. We do not request Apple's cross-app tracking permission. Separately, optional product analytics starts only after your explicit in-app consent. We do not sell your personal data.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete a saved person at any time inside the app, which removes their stored details and cached readings. Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local data from your device. AI providers may retain request logs for a limited period under their own policies for abuse prevention and service operation.

7. Children

AstroMation is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children under 13.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because we do not hold your birth data on our servers, you can exercise these directly on your device. For any question about this policy, contact us at info@machinity.ai.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will revise the “Last updated” date above when we do.
