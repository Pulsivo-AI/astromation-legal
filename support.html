Frequently asked

How is my birth chart calculated?

Entirely on your device, using a professional astronomy engine. Your birth data stays on your phone by default.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited daily readings and the Ask consultation chat. The natal birth chart is free for everyone. Premium is an auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly); manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings (the "More" tab) and tap "Restore purchase", or use "Restore" on the subscription screen.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AstroMation → Cancel.
